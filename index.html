A simple bridge to your Mac.

01

Continue conversations

Keep a separate Siri Chat conversation for each paired phone, and ask follow-up questions without starting over.

02

Send photos and receipts

Review and submit an image from your phone. Your paired Mac performs local OCR and sensitive-information checks before Siri Chat.

03

Connect directly

Your phone connects to your Mac over HTTPS on your Tailscale network. Minmi does not run a cloud service for answers.

PAIR ONCE

Your Mac stays in control.

Install Minmi Mac, show a short-lived pairing QR code, then scan it from the phone app. Requests are handled by the Mac you paired.
